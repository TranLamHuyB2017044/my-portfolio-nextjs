An AI-driven mobile application designed to accelerate digital transformation. The primary focus of the app is advanced OCR (Optical Character Recognition) capabilities tailored for Vietnamese documents. It effortlessly extracts text and structured information from citizen identity cards (CCCD), driver's licenses, vehicle registrations, and retail receipts. Users can conveniently copy the extracted text, upload it to Google Drive, or share it across various productivity apps.

In addition to OCR, the app features a powerful RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chat interface. Users can upload their own documents, and the system leverages Qdrant Vector DB alongside OpenAI APIs to generate summaries and answer natural language queries based on the document's content. The entire ecosystem is powered by a robust Python/Flask backend utilizing VietOCR for vision tasks and MongoDB for data persistence, paired with a smooth Flutter mobile frontend.
