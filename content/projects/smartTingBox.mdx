Smart Ting Box is a comprehensive sales management application designed to handle accurate expense statistics and revenue calculations for businesses of all scales—from small retail shops to larger enterprises.

Beyond standard point-of-sale features, the app integrates cutting-edge smart functionalities. It utilizes a computer vision-powered product recognition camera to instantly identify items, eliminating the need for manual barcode scanning and significantly speeding up the checkout process. Additionally, it seamlessly connects with IoT hardware to trigger smart speaker notifications the moment a payment is successfully processed, ensuring clear and immediate feedback for both cashiers and customers. Built with Flutter, Node.js, and Python, the system delivers a fast, cross-platform, and highly reliable operational experience.
